# Draft privacy policy — LibreShelf
LibreShelf indexes books selected through Android's system file picker and stores titles, authors, document references, cover thumbnails and settings on the device. It does not request internet access or send this information to a server. Tapping a book grants the chosen external reader read access to that document; that reader operates under its own privacy policy. Android settings can clear the app's local data and uninstalling removes its private index. Original books are not deleted by LibreShelf. Before publication, add publisher identity, contact details, effective date, and verify this policy against the final release and dependencies.
